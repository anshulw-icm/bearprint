What is Bearprint?

Bearprint tracks market breadth from the loss side — every day it measures what proportion of NSE-listed stocks closed below their previous price. Instead of asking "how much did the market fall?", it asks "how many stocks fell?"

A headline Nifty drop of 1% could be driven by two large-caps. Bearprint cuts through that noise. A reading of 75 means 75% of all NSE equity stocks closed red — broadcast market pain, not concentrated.

Bearprint = (stocks closing below prev. close ÷ total NSE EQ stocks) × 100
