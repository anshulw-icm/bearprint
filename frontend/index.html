About the index

What is Bearprint?

Bearprint is a market breadth index that measures how widespread losses are across the Nifty 500 on any given trading day. Rather than tracking how much the market fell, it tracks how many stocks fell.

When the headline index drops 1%, it could be driven by two large-caps. Bearprint cuts through that noise — a reading of 68 means 68% of all tracked stocks closed red. That's a very different picture.

Bearprint = (stocks closing below previous close ÷ 500) × 100

Rising Bearprint over consecutive sessions often signals broad market deterioration — sometimes before the Nifty 50 visibly reflects it.
