What is Bearprint?

Bearprint measures how widespread losses are across all NSE-listed stocks each day. Instead of asking "how much did the market fall?" — it asks "how many stocks fell?"

A 1% Nifty drop could be two large-caps dragging the index. A Bearprint of 75 means 75% of all NSE equity stocks closed red — that's a very different picture.

Bearprint = (stocks closing below prev. close ÷ total NSE EQ stocks) × 100
